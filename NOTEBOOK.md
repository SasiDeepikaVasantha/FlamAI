# Experimental Notebook

Record experiments chronologically. Do not back-fill only successful experiments.

## Experiment template

### Date
YYYY-MM-DD

### Hypothesis
What do I think is wrong?

### Experiment
What exact code/command isolates the issue?

### Result
Record the actual output.

### Interpretation
What does the result prove or fail to prove?

### Decision
Keep, reject, or revise the hypothesis.
